# Teardown guide

Use these lists to label each competitor ad the same way. Consistent labels make the pattern table work. The last column says which Sloosh skill can make our own version. The producing skill always gives the real quote before any paid work.

## Formats: scalers (reach cold audiences)

| Format | What to look for | Our version made with |
|---|---|---|
| Founder or creator to camera | One person talks to the lens, phone-shot look | ugc-storyboard, or UGC video ad in the Sloosh Ads pack, from our own founder's or creator's photo with permission |
| Partnership or creator ad | The post runs from a creator's handle | ugc-storyboard, with our own creator and their permission |
| Education-first video | Explains a problem before the product | product-video clips planned with ugc-storyboard |
| Grid static | Many products, or one product shown many ways | ad-variants, or an ad grid Space through published-spaces |
| Authority | An expert fronts the ad | Only with our own real expert, their permission and their approval of the script. Never fabricate an expert. |
| Small brand against the giant | A villain such as the usual way or a category | ad-variants, or comparison ad in the Sloosh Ads pack, against a category only |
| Catalog or product set | Several products in one clean frame | product-imagery |

## Formats: supporting cast (convert mid-funnel)

| Format | What to look for | Our version made with |
|---|---|---|
| Headline statement | One bold line, product hero | ad-variants |
| Us against the usual way | Two columns or a split frame | ad-variants, or comparison ad in the Sloosh Ads pack |
| Stat callout | A number as the hero | ad-variants, with our own sourced number only |
| Before and after | Two frames of a change | before and after ad in the Sloosh Ads pack, with truthful frames |
| Testimonial or review card | A quote, stars or a name | testimonial ads in the Sloosh Ads pack, with our real quotes and permission |
| Feature callout | Arrows or labels on the product | ad-variants; the motion version with product-video |
| Offer or sale banner | A price, a discount or a deadline | sale banner set in the Sloosh Ads pack |
| Product in hand | A hand holds the product, casual light | ad-variants, or a product-in-hand Space through published-spaces |
| Unboxing | Packaging opens on camera | product-video, or an unboxing Space through published-spaces |
| Seasonal refresh | A holiday or season set | ad-variants |

To check that a Space exists for a format, hand off to the published-spaces skill. Ask it to search for words such as ad grid, product in hand or unboxing. It chooses by the Space description and runs it.

## Hook families

- Curiosity gap
- Bold specific claim
- First-person confession
- Contrast, or before and after
- POV relatability
- The buyer's exact question
- Countdown or challenge

A video hook has three parts: the visual action, the spoken line and the caption. A static hook has two parts: the visual and the headline. Record each part separately.

## Proof types

- Stat: a number. Note if the ad shows a source. Do not judge if the number is true.
- Review: a quote, stars or a count.
- Demo: the product works on camera.
- Authority: an expert, a lab or a press mention.
- None: the ad relies on feeling or offer.

## Reading the likely funnel role

- Likely scaler: broad hook, no assumed product knowledge, a person or a story leads.
- Likely supporting cast: names the product early, answers an objection, shows an offer or proof.
- Write "likely". You see the ad, not its targeting or results.

## Run length as a signal

- Tier 3: a paid competitor ad live for 60 days or more. Treat it as a strong signal.
- Tier 4: a competitor's organic post that took off.
- A paid ad under 60 days or of unknown run length is weaker than tier 3. Say so and rank it below the tier 3 seeds.
- Run length is not proof. Do not say an ad "works" or "fails".

## Decayed or risky patterns

Never use these formats for our brand: fake chat screenshots, invented reviews, celebrity look-alikes, "doctor" avatars with no real doctor and press-logo walls without the press. If a competitor ad uses one of these formats, name the format in neutral words, such as "a chat-screenshot format", "a review card with no source shown" or "a person in a lab coat". Do not say whether it is real, invented or true. Do not seed it for our brand. Use real sources or a different format.

## Words to avoid in a teardown

Do not write that a competitor lies, cheats, copies, misleads or breaks a rule. Do not guess their spend, sales or return on ad spend. Write what the ad shows and stop there.
