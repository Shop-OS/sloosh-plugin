# Asset matrix

Use this table to pick the producing skill and the cost tier for each asset in the plan. The tiers are rough. The producing skill gives the real quote before any paid work.

Core skills come with the Sloosh plugin. A pack skill works only when that pack is installed. If a pack is not installed, name the pack in plain words and give the asset as a manual task.

Cost tiers: none (no paid work), low (one or two image rounds), medium (short video clips, or images and video together), high (a multi-clip film or a batch across a catalog), varies (a Space run, priced by the Space).

## Channel defaults

Use these as a starting list. Cut any asset that has no job in this campaign.

- Store: hero banner, hero stills, one product clip.
- Paid social: 6 to 12 ad statics in 4:5 and 9:16, a hook test grid, one UGC-style ad.
- Organic social: a carousel, a reveal clip, a short reel from the Reel, TikTok or Short row.
- Short video: 9:16 clips from the Reel, TikTok or Short row, with captions. Keep the product and the text out of the top 14%, the bottom 35% and 6% at each side.
- Email: a hero banner for each send, and the hero stills.
- Marketplaces: a listing set.
