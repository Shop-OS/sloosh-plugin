# The territory card

Write each card in this shape. Paste the chosen card into the producing skill's request. The example is for a scalp serum.

```text
Territory B · "Scalp first"
Idea: hair fall is a scalp problem; show the scalp, never the hair.
World: macro, clinical daylight, black slate and white ceramic, deep forest green accents, slow push-ins.
Hook family: specific claim + exact question.
Proof: dermatologist-tested (claim, with source); 14 reviews say "itch gone in a week" (language evidence).
Deliverables: 3 macro heroes (product-imagery), 6 statics in 4:5 and 9:16 (ad-variants), 1 macro clip of 10 s (product-video).
Cost tier: medium. Risk: macro shots may drift the label; keep a label close-up as a reference.
```

## Field notes

- Name: two words the team can say in a meeting.
- Idea: one line. If it needs two, it is two territories.
- World: place, light, palette, type feel and pace. Name colours in words unless the brand kit gives a hex code.
- Hook family: the opening move, such as a specific claim, an exact question, a POV moment, a contrast or a ritual.
- Proof: each line is claim evidence (a measured result with a source) or language evidence (the buyer's own words). A complaint supports a pain angle. It never supports a number.
- Deliverables: each item names its producing skill, its ratio and its count.
- Cost tier: low, medium, high or varies. The producing skill gives the real quote.

## Cost tiers

- Low: stills only.
- Medium: stills and one short clip.
- High: a multi-shot film or a code-rendered launch film.
- Varies: a Space run. The Space gives its own quote.
- Risk: the one thing most likely to go wrong in production, and the guard against it.

## Shapes that differ in kind

- Proof-led: the mechanism, the ingredient or the measured result is the hero.
- Feeling-led: the moment after use, the mood the buyer wants.
- Product as object: material, form and detail, shot like a design piece.
- Creator-led: a real founder or creator talks to camera, with their consent.
- Culture or season-led: a festival, a season or a cultural moment frames the product.
