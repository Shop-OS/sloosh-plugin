I pulled six ads a rival oat-milk creamer brand has been running for about three months. Here is what each one shows:

1. A founder talks to camera in her kitchen: "I quit dairy and hated every creamer I tried."
2. A static: two columns, their carton on the right, a plain grey "regular creamer" carton on the left, with a short ingredient list.
3. A static: one bold line, "Froths like the real thing", carton on a cream background.
4. A reel: hands pour the creamer into coffee in slow motion, captions only, no voice.
5. Another founder-to-camera video in the same kitchen, this time answering "does it curdle?"
6. A static with a 4.8-star review card and a short customer quote.

What are they doing, what keeps repeating, and how do we make our own version for our creamer without copying them?
