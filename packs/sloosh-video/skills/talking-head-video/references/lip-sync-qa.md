# Lip-sync QA and prompt pattern

Use this file with the `talking-head-video` skill.

## Prompt pattern for a clip made from a photo

Write one prompt in this order:

1. The subject: the person from the reference image, medium close-up, at eye level.
2. The action: the person looks at the camera and says the script, in quotes, word for word.
3. The delivery: natural pauses, small head movement, a calm pace.
4. The look: soft indoor light, a plain background.
5. The guard: keep the face, hair and clothes exactly as the reference.
6. The sound: the spoken line only, with no music.
7. The frame: no on-screen text, no logos, no added people.

Example: "The person from the reference image, medium close-up at eye level, looks at the camera and says: \"Most oat milk tastes like water. Ours pours thick.\" Natural pauses, small head movement, soft indoor light, plain background. Keep the face exactly as the reference. Spoken line only, no music. No on-screen text."

## Script rules for speech

- Use short sentences with one idea each. Use contractions.
- Name the product once. Put the call to action last.
- Put the hook in the first 3 seconds. Say the point first, then the reason.
- Plan about 150 words a minute. A 15-second clip holds about 35 to 40 words.
- Spell out numbers and names the way the person says them.
- Fast speech makes the lips drift. Use fewer words for the same length.

## Checklist before delivery

| Check | Pass when |
|---|---|
| Lip sync | The lips match the words through the whole clip, also on the last line. |
| Complete words | Every word is there. The first and last words are not clipped. |
| No invented words | The clip says only the approved script. |
| Likeness | The face, hair and clothes match the source. Nothing is added to the face. |
| Eyes and head | The eyes stay on the lens. The head moves a little, not in a loop. |
| Dub | The language, the names and the product name are correct. |
| Audio | One voice only, no music, no echo, no cut at the end. |
| Frame | No warped teeth, hands or background. No on-screen text unless asked. |

## When a check fails

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Lips drift on fast speech | Cut words. Keep the same length. |
| A clipped first or last word | Add a short pause before the first word and after the last word. |
| Likeness is off | Ask for a sharper, front-facing clip or photo with even light. |
| A word is wrong in a dub | Spell the name the way it sounds in that language. |

Each fix needs a new quote, a new approval and a new key.
