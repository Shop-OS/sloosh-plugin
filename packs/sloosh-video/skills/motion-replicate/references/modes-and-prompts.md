# Modes, instructions and checks

## Choose the mode

| The user wants | Mode | What to search for |
|---|---|---|
| The same moves in a new world, with a new character, creator or mascot | Motion transfer | motion transfer, motion replicate |
| The same clip, with only the product or one object replaced | Subject swap (object) | object swap, product swap |
| The same clip, with one person replaced by the user's character or creator | Subject swap (person) | character swap, trending video |
| A portrait and a motion clip turned into a vertical character video | Motion transfer | character motion, motion replicate |
| A product and a reference ad turned into a finished ad of the same rhythm | Motion transfer | product ad from reference video |

- Use motion transfer when the setting should change. Use subject swap when only one element changes.
- Motion transfer holds identity better than a swap when the subject photo is weak.
- A swap needs a subject of about the same size and shape as the one it replaces. A tall bottle does not replace a small tube well.

## Subject images

- Use a sharp, front-facing, well-lit photo of the character, creator or mascot. Show the full body when the moves use the full body.
- For a product, use the front and a close-up of the label.
- Add a background image only when the user wants a fixed setting.

## Instructions field or prompt

Name every image by its role. Say what stays and what changes. Give one setting. Ask for no text.

Motion transfer:

```
Copy the motion, timing and camera of the reference video exactly. The character is the mascot from the character image. The setting is a sunlit kitchen. Keep the character's colours and proportions. No on-screen text, logos or watermarks.
```

Subject swap, object. Use this prompt only when the user has rights to the whole clip, such as footage they filmed, and every person who stays agreed to appear. Otherwise use motion transfer.

```
Keep the reference video exactly: the people, the setting, the motion and the camera. Replace only the bottle in the person's right hand with the product from the product image, at the same size and angle. Keep the product label readable and its colours exact. Remove every logo and line of text of the original product.
```

Subject swap, person:

```
Keep the setting, the motion and the camera of the reference video. Replace the dancer with the person from the subject image. Keep that person's face, hair and outfit. Remove the original dancer completely. No on-screen text.
```

## Craft rules

- Keep the reference length when the Space or model allows it. If the hold is more than the allowance left, offer a shorter cut of the strongest moves.
- Keep the reference aspect ratio when you can. Use 9:16 for Reels, Stories and TikTok.
- For 9:16 placements, keep the product and any burned-in text out of the top 14%, the bottom 35% and 6% at each side of the frame.
- In motion transfer, use the reference only for its motion.
- A subject swap keeps the rest of the clip. Use it only when the user has rights to the whole clip and every person who stays agreed to appear.
- In every mode, do not keep the reference's logo, on-screen text, watermark, music or voice.

## Check the result

- The timing and the moves match the reference.
- The subject keeps its identity: face, colours, proportions.
- The product label is readable and the colours match.
- After a swap, nothing of the original subject remains, and only people who agreed to appear stay in the clip.
- No logo, watermark or text of the original creator or brand remains.
- The clip could not pass as the original creator's or brand's video.
