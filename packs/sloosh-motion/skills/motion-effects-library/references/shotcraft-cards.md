# Shotcraft cards

124 named shots for product, brand and interface films, in ten groups. Each card is one shot or one shot pattern. The descriptions are our own short English summaries of the video-shotcraft cards (Apache-2.0). See `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` in this folder.

## How to read a row

- Card: the name to ask for.
- Looks like: what the viewer sees.
- Use it for: the moment it suits.
- Length: the usual length of the shot. Most cards run at 30 fps, so 30 frames is 1 s. "Pattern" means the card is a technique that takes its frames from the shots next to it.
- Made with: the sloosh-motion skill that builds it.

A card with "two moves" or "three moves" holds several variants. Ask for the variant by its name.

## Sequence tip

A promo usually rises in energy: a calm opener, a camera or interface section, faster transitions and type in the middle, one big rhythm moment, then a calm outro. Use each high-energy card at most once per film.
