# Clip craft for Reels, TikTok and Shorts

Use this file to plan the hook, write the prompt and check the clip. It costs nothing to read.

## Hook types

Write three hooks. Use one of each type. Let the user pick one.

| Type | What it does | Example for a candle |
|---|---|---|
| Curiosity gap | Shows a moment and holds back the reason. | The lid lifts and the room goes quiet. |
| Bold claim | States one benefit the user can prove. | One candle, the whole flat smells of cedar. |
| Point of view | Puts the viewer in a real moment. | POV: you finally light the good candle. |

Rules for every hook:

- Open on a visual action in the first second. A spoken line alone is not a hook.
- The visual action, the spoken or on-screen line and the caption say different things. They must not repeat each other.
- Show the product by second 3.
- Use only claims the user gives or the product page shows.

## Beats

Plan the clip in two or three beats.

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0 to 3 seconds | The hook: a visual action close on the product. |
| 3 seconds to the last 2 | One use moment or detail, such as a hand, a pour or a label close-up. |
| Last 2 seconds | The product still and centred, with room for the call to action caption. |

Default to 9 seconds. Keep the clip at 15 seconds or less. Use a length that the chosen model lists.

## Prompt template

Fill the brackets. Keep the order.

"Vertical 9:16, [length] seconds. 0 to 3 seconds: [visual action, such as the lid lifts and a thin line of smoke rises], close on the product from reference 1, handheld phone feel. 3 to [n] seconds: [beat, such as a hand turns the jar so the label reads]. Last 2 seconds: the product centred and still, with clear space for a caption. Keep the product, its label and its colours exactly as in reference 1. Keep the subject in the centre of the frame. No on-screen text. Sound: [room tone and one real sound, such as the match strike], no music."

- Ask for no on-screen text. Captions come from a captions Space after the clip.
- Ask for no music when a soundtrack Space will add music later.
- If the user wants a spoken line, write it in the first person, short and casual.
- Drop the Sound line when the model lists no audio.

## Safe zone for 9:16

Keep the product and any text out of these parts of the frame:

- The top 14%.
- The bottom 35%.
- 6% at each side.

The platform's buttons, captions and profile name cover these parts.

## Checklist for the user to check the video

- The hook lands by second 3.
- The product shows by second 3 and matches the reference: shape, label and colours.
- The call to action is in the last 2 seconds.
- No text is invented in the frame.
- Caption words are spelled right and match any speech.
- Music, if any, sits under the speech and fits the pace.
- The product and text stay inside the safe zone.
- The clip is 15 seconds or less.

## Fixes

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The model adds text to the frame | Ask again for no on-screen text. Add words with a captions Space. |
| The hook is unclear | Write a stronger visual action for the first second. |
| The music fights the pace | Run the soundtrack Space again with a clearer mood, such as calm with no drums. |
| The clip is too long | Make a shorter clip with a new quote. |
