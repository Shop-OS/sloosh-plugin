# Virality checklist

These eight signals come from the ranked virality criteria in AI-Youtube-Shorts-Generator (MIT License), which uses them to find the moments in a long video most likely to work as a short. Here they score a script before any shot is made. They are in order of impact.

| # | Signal | What it is | A script line that has it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | A first line that makes the viewer curious at once. | "Nobody tells you this about lunch boxes." |
| 2 | Emotional peak | A real reaction: surprise, laughter, relief, frustration or excitement. | The parent's face when the bag comes out of the dishwasher clean. |
| 3 | Opinion | A strong or unexpected view that viewers agree or disagree with. | "Sandwich bags are the worst thing in your kitchen." |
| 4 | Revelation | A surprising fact, number or confession that changes how the viewer sees it. | "We threw away 400 bags last year." |
| 5 | Conflict | A problem met head-on, or pushback the script answers. | "I said it would never seal. It sealed." |
| 6 | Quotable | One line that works on its own, as a caption or a quote card. | "One bag, all term." |
| 7 | Story | The payoff or twist of a short story. | The bin that was full every week is now half empty. |
| 8 | Practical value | A tip, a hack or a fact the viewer can use straight away. | "Turn it inside out to dry it in a minute." |

## Scoring

- Give each signal 0, 1 or 2. 0: not in the script. 1: present but weak or late. 2: clear and early enough to matter.
- Add the scores for a total out of 16. Show each score with a short reason.
- The hook must score 2. If it does not, rewrite the hook first.
- A script does not need all eight. A 15 second script that scores well on three or four signals is strong. Choose the signals that fit the goal:
    - Awareness: hook, emotional peak, opinion, quotable.
    - Consideration: revelation, conflict, practical value.
    - A sale or a launch: hook, revelation, practical value, and a story when there is time.
- Use a number or a result only when the user gave it. A revelation without evidence becomes a confession or a question, not a statistic.
- Score the first draft, revise once, then score again. Show both totals. The total is a self-check, not a forecast of views.

## Hook moves

Use a different move for each of the three alternative hooks.

- Curiosity: "The secret is...", "Nobody talks about..."
- Confession: "I was completely wrong about..."
- Opinion: a strong, short claim the viewer can disagree with.
- Revelation: the most surprising true fact the user gave.
- POV: "POV: it's 7 a.m. and the lunch boxes are empty."
- Problem: the moment the problem hurts, shown before it is named.
- Result first: the end state, then "here's how".
