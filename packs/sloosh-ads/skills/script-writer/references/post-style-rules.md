# Post-style rules

The structure and content rules below are adapted from the post-writing prompts in langchain-ai social-media-agent (MIT License). There they shape a social post. Here they shape the spoken and on-screen lines of a short video script.

## Structure

- Hook: short and to the point. Keep the on-screen hook to 5 words or fewer. The spoken hook can be a little longer, up to about 10 words.
- Body: the main point in short sentences. One idea for each beat. No more than three short sentences of voiceover in a row without a new visual.
- Call to action: 3 to 6 words, with one verb.

## Content rules

- Say what the product does and what changes for the buyer. Do not describe features the buyer cannot see or feel.
- Keep it simple enough for anyone in the audience to follow. Keep one concrete detail in each beat, such as a number, a texture or a moment.
- Keep every line short, concrete and easy to say out loud.
- Write as a person talking to another person: casual and friendly, not formal and not a sales voice.
- Use the present tense, so the news feels immediate: "It just goes in the dishwasher", not "It will go in the dishwasher".
- Do not put hashtags in the on-screen text or the voiceover. Hashtags belong in the post text, which the ad copy skill writes.
- Use at most one emoji, only in on-screen text, and only when the brand kit's voice allows it.
- Name the action in the call to action: what to tap, where to go or what to buy. If the user gave a link or a store name, use it there.

## Beat counts and word budgets

Spoken lines run at about 2.5 words a second. Leave the first beat and the call to action room to breathe.

| Length | Beats, with the hook | Voiceover words, at most | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 s | 3 to 4 | 35 | 3 cards or fewer |
| 30 s | 4 to 6 | 70 | 5 cards or fewer |
| 60 s | 6 to 9 | 140 | 8 cards or fewer |

Keep each on-screen card to 6 words or fewer. For 9:16 placements, plan the on-screen text for the middle of the frame: keep it out of the top 14%, the bottom 35% and 6% at each side.

## Post-style check

Run this check on the revised script.

- The hook's three parts do not repeat each other.
- The product appears by second 3.
- The call to action is in the last 2 seconds of a 15 second script and the last 3 seconds of a longer one.
- The voiceover fits the word budget for the length.
- No hashtags, no more than one emoji, present tense, no sales voice.
- Every claim, number and offer came from the user.
- Every "Script:" rule in the brand kit is followed.
