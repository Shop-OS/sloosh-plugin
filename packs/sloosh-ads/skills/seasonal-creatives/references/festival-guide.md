# Festival and season guide

Motifs, light and palette for each occasion, with what to avoid. Use the brand palette for backgrounds. Use the festival palette for props and light. This guide is a starting point, not a cultural authority. When the brand serves a specific community or region, confirm the symbols, the greeting and the date with the user.

| Occasion | Motifs and props | Light | Palette for props | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Diwali | clay diyas, marigold garlands, rangoli, brass trays, sparklers, gift boxes | warm candle light, bokeh fairy lights, dusk | saffron, gold, deep indigo, magenta | fireworks over the product, religious icons unless the brand uses them, crowded sets |
| Holi | gulal powder clouds, water splashes, white kurtas, pichkari | bright daylight, backlit powder | pink, yellow, green, turquoise | powder over the label, muddy mixed colours |
| Raksha Bandhan | rakhi threads, sweets, thali, hands tying a rakhi | soft indoor daylight | red, gold, cream | staged family stock poses |
| Eid | crescent and star lanterns, dates, arches, embroidered textiles | cool blue dusk to warm lantern light | emerald, navy, gold, ivory | a wrong greeting (use "Eid Mubarak"), alcohol |
| Christmas | pine, ornaments, ribbon, snow, wrapped gifts, warm wood | warm tungsten, snowy window light | forest green, red, gold, cream | Santa with the product unless it is on brand, glitter everywhere |
| New Year | confetti, a clock face, sparkle, a midnight skyline | cool night, neon accents | black, gold, silver | a wrong year (check every digit) |
| Valentine's Day | roses, heart-shaped shadows, handwritten notes, two cups | soft pink window light | blush, red, cream | hearts over the product |
| Mother's Day | flowers, a breakfast tray, a handwritten card | morning daylight | pastels, white | generic family stock faces |
| Halloween | pumpkins, cobwebs, candles, fog | low orange and purple light | orange, black, violet | gore; keep it playful |
| Black Friday and Cyber Monday | blank tags, a dark set, a spotlight | hard spotlight, high contrast | black, white, one accent | any price or discount the user did not give, a discount-store look unless the brand is one |
| Summer | citrus, water drops, linen, sand, pool tiles | hard sun, dappled shade | aqua, lemon, coral, white | melting or sweating products |
| Monsoon | rain on glass, umbrellas, chai, wet leaves | soft overcast light | teal, grey, green | water on electronics |
| Winter | wool, steam, frost on glass, a fireplace | warm interior against a cool window | camel, cream, charcoal | snow indoors |
| Back to school | notebooks, desks, lockers, bright primaries | flat bright daylight | yellow, blue, red | childish props for adult products |

For Black Friday offer banners, offer the `sale-banner-set` skill.

## Prompt shape

"The [product] from reference 1, label facing the camera, on [a prop surface] with [two or three motifs] around it, a [backdrop colour] backdrop, [light from the table]. Eye level, product about half the frame, empty top quarter for a headline. Festive but calm, true colours, no text. Keep the label, cap and proportions exactly as in reference 1."

- For a greeting or offer image, replace no text with the exact words in quotes and say where they sit, for example "Eid Mubarak" in the empty top quarter. A WhatsApp greeting is a 1:1 still.
- Swap the props, backdrop and light from the occasion's row.
- Use the brand palette for backgrounds. Use the festival palette for props and light.
- If the palette leaks into the product, start the prompt with "the product keeps its exact colours" and use fewer palette words.
- Use a few motifs, not a crowded set. Keep them off the label.
- If a symbol looks wrong or cliched, ask the user and use the brand's own motifs.

## Text in images

- Latin script: keep the words short. Pick a model whose description says it renders text well. Read every letter of the result.
- Devanagari, Arabic, Tamil and other scripts: results vary. Check every glyph. If one glyph is wrong, use the greeting in English, or ask the user for the greeting as an image from their design tool. To use that image, add it as a reference, quote again, ask for a new approval and use a new key.
- Dates and years: check every digit before you deliver.
- An offer, a discount or a date comes only from the user, word for word.

## Clip ideas (about 6 seconds, ambient motion, from an approved still)

- Diwali: diya flames flicker and a sparkler enters the frame.
- Christmas: snow drifts past the window and an ornament sways.
- Holi: a slow-motion puff of powder rises behind the product.
- Summer: condensation runs down the bottle.
- Monsoon: rain streaks the glass and the product stays sharp behind it.

Keep the product still and sharp. Move only the props, the light and the air around it.
