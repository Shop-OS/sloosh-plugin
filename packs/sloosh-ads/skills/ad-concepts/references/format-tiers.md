# Ad format tiers

The ranking question for each format: does it open cold, new audiences (a scaler), or does it convert people who are already mid-funnel (supporting cast)? A good portfolio has both. The last columns say which Sloosh skill makes the format and its rough cost tier. The producing skill always gives the real quote before any paid work.

## Scalers (reach cold audiences)

| Format | Why it scales | Made with | Cost tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder or creator to camera | The person does the targeting | ugc-video-ad or ugc-storyboard, from the founder's or creator's own photo with permission | varies through a Space, or medium for storyboard clips |
| Partnership or creator ad | Starts from the creator's audience | ugc-video-ad with the creator's photo and permission | varies through a Space |
| Education-first video | Works in categories that need explaining | product-video clips planned with ugc-storyboard | medium |
| Grid static (many products, or one product many ways) | Cheap and proven at scale | ad-variants, or an ad grid Space through published-spaces | low, or varies through a Space |
| Authority (an expert fronts it) | Trust-gated niches | ugc-video-ad, from the expert's own photo or clip, with their permission and their approval of the script. Never fabricate an expert. | varies through a Space |
| Small brand against the giant | A clear villain, such as the usual way or a category, not a named rival | comparison-ad or ad-variants | low, or varies through a Space |
| Catalog or product set | Works at the top of the funnel too | product-imagery | low |

## Supporting cast (convert mid-funnel)

| Format | Made with | Cost tier |
|---|---|---|
| Headline statement (one bold line, product hero) | ad-variants | low |
| Us against the usual way or a category | comparison-ad | low, or varies through a Space |
| Stat callout | ad-variants, with a sourced number only | low |
| Before and after | before-after-ad | low, or varies through a Space |
| Testimonial or review card | testimonial-ads, with real quotes and permission | low, or varies through a Space |
| Feature callout | ad-variants; the motion version with product-video | low to medium |
| Offer or sale banner | sale-banner-set | low, or varies through a Space |
| Product in hand | ad-variants, or a product-in-hand Space through published-spaces | low, or varies through a Space |
| Unboxing | product-video, or an unboxing Space through published-spaces | medium, or varies through a Space |
| Borrowed proven layout | reference-ad-remix | low, or varies through a Space |
| Seasonal refresh | ad-variants | low |

To check that a Space exists for a format, search `list_spaces` for words such as ad grid, product in hand or unboxing. Choose by its description. A Space run is priced by the Space, so its cost tier varies. A route that can go through a Space is "low, or varies through a Space".

## Decayed or risky

Fake chat screenshots, invented reviews, celebrity look-alikes, "doctor" avatars with no real doctor and press-logo walls without the press. Sloosh can render them. These skills refuse them. Use real sources or a different format.

## Hook families (one per concept; rotate them)

- Curiosity gap
- Bold specific claim
- First-person confession
- Contrast, or before and after
- POV relatability
- The buyer's exact question
- Countdown or challenge

A video hook has three parts: the visual action, the spoken line and the caption. A static hook has two parts: the visual and the headline. The parts must not say the same thing.

## The awareness stage picks the treatment

| Stage | Treatment | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | A feeling, a conversation | Learn about the situation |
| Problem-aware | The pain and the outcome, friction anchored in time | See how |
| Solution-aware | A change of state, a documented statistic | Compare, see a demo |
| Product-aware | A statistic, an objection and its proof | Try, buy |
