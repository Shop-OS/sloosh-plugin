# Placement sizes for reframing

Use the brand kit's channels and aspect ratios first. Use this table only when the kit has none or the user names a placement.

| Placement | Aspect ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram and Facebook feed | 4:5 or 1:1 | 4:5 fills more of the feed. |
| Reels, Stories and TikTok | 9:16 | Keep the product and any text out of the top 14%, the bottom 35% and 6% at each side of the frame. |
| YouTube, website hero and landscape banner | 16:9 | Leave room on one side for a headline if the user adds one later. |
| Wide website banner | 21:9 | Keep the subject near the centre third. Very wide frames add a lot of new area. |
| Pinterest | 2:3 | Extend upward and downward. |
| Google landscape display | 1.91:1 | If the model has no 1.91:1, use 16:9 and tell the user to crop. |
| Product page and marketplace | 1:1 | Keep the product centred with an even margin. |

## How much new area a change adds

- 4:5 to 1:1, or 1:1 to 4:5: a small extension. One step is enough.
- 1:1 to 9:16, or 16:9 to 1:1: a large extension. Describe what the new area continues.
- 16:9 to 9:16, or the reverse: most of the frame is new. Try one step first. If the subject repeats or a seam shows, go in two steps through 1:1 or 4:5, and check each step. Each step is a new quote, a new approval and a new key.
