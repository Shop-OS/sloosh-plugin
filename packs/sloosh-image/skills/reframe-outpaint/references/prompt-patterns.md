# Prompt patterns for reframing

Write one prompt for each target ratio. Pass the source image as a reference asset.

## Photo, outpainted

"Extend the photo in the reference image to a 9:16 frame. Keep the original image unchanged at its original scale, in the lower two thirds. Fill the new area by continuing the marble surface and the soft grey backdrop. Keep the same light and perspective. Add no new objects and no text."

## Finished design, recomposed

"Recompose the ad in the reference image for 16:9. Keep the product, the headline text exactly as written, the colours and the style. Put the product on the left and the headline on the right. Extend the background."

## Rules

- Name what the new area continues: the surface, the wall, the sky or the light.
- Say "no new objects" unless the user asked for one.
- Say where the original sits in the new frame: centred, in the lower two thirds or on one side.
- For a design, write the headline out exactly as it is in the source.
- For a large change, such as 16:9 to 9:16, try one step first. If the subject repeats or a seam shows, go in two steps through 1:1 or 4:5. Each step is a new quote, a new approval and a new key.

## Fixes after a weak result

- A seam shows: add "continue the exact surface texture".
- A seam shows at the old edge: ask the user to crop a few pixels off that edge, then extend again.
- The subject repeats: add "do not repeat the product".
- Text broke in a design: write the headline out exactly and ask for it unchanged.
