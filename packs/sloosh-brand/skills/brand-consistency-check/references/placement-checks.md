# Placement checks

Use these checks when the user says where an image will run. Platforms change their limits. Tell the user to confirm the current limits before a large launch.

## Ratio and interface zones

| Placement | Ratio | Keep the product and text out of |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram or Facebook feed | 4:5 (1:1 is the fallback) | No fixed zone; keep text and the logo off the edges |
| Reels, Stories, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Snapchat | 9:16 | The top 14%, the bottom 35% and 6% at each side |
| Facebook, LinkedIn or X feed | 1:1 | No fixed zone; keep a small margin at each edge |
| YouTube, landscape display, website hero | 16:9 | The bottom of the frame on a video player, where the controls sit |
| YouTube thumbnail | 16:9 | The bottom-right corner, where the length label sits |
| Pinterest pin | 2:3 | No fixed zone; keep text light |
| Email or messaging header | 3:1 or 21:9 | Keep the copy short and the product at the left or the centre |
| Marketplace main image | 1:1 | Pure white background, product fills most of the frame, no text, no badges, no props, no watermark |
| Product page gallery | 1:1 or 4:5 | Use the same crop and product size on every product in the set |
| LinkedIn banner | 4:1 | The lower left on a profile, where the profile photo sits |

A wrong ratio is a fix, not a drop, when the subject has room around it. A crop that cuts the product or the logo is a drop for that placement.

## Text on the image

- Feed static: a headline of six words or fewer, one supporting line and one call to action.
- Story or Reel cover: four words or fewer.
- Thumbnail: three to five words in high contrast, readable at phone size.
- Every word is spelled right. Check each letter of the brand name and the product name.
- Small text that cannot be read on a phone is a fix.

## Brand checks that depend on the placement

- Logo: a feed or story ad shows the logo where the brand kit needs it. A marketplace main image shows the logo only on the product itself.
- Colour: a dark interface (Stories, Reels) can make a dark brand colour disappear at the edges. Check the logo and text contrast against the background.
- Claims: a before and after image needs truthful frames of the same product or person. A testimonial needs a real quote and the customer's permission. A number needs a source.

## Video

- There is no video preview. Check only a still or a screenshot of one frame that the user gives. Check that frame for palette, logo and the interface zones.
- Say that motion, audio, captions and later frames were not checked.
- On a paid placement the product should be clear early and the end frame should carry the offer and the call to action. Ask the user to confirm this when you cannot see it.
